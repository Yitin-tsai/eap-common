# eap-common - LLM Context

Shared compile-time contracts only; no runnable service and no business-state ownership.

## Core Event Schemas

- `OrderSubmittedEvent`: `orderId`, `userId`, `marketId`, `marketSequence`, price, amount, side, timestamp.
- `OrderConfirmedEvent`: preserves the same order identity, market sequence, price, amount, side, and timestamp after reservation.
- `OrderFailedEvent`: order/user identity, reason, failure type, timestamp.
- `TradeExecutedEvent`: `tradeId`, execution sequence, market, buyer/seller users and orders, both market sequences, origin prices, deal price, quantity, timestamp.

`OrderTradeAppliedEvent` and `WalletTradeSettledEvent` are retired. Do not reintroduce a MatchEngine completion callback without a new architecture decision.

TDA contracts: `AuctionCreatedEvent`, `AuctionBidSubmittedEvent`, `AuctionBidConfirmedEvent`, and `AuctionClearedEvent` on `auction.exchange`. Only Wallet's bid confirmation currently uses an outbox; Order bid submission and MatchEngine create/clear events publish directly. Do not inherit CDA reliability or capacity claims.

RabbitMQ constants live at `src/main/java/com/eap/common/constants/RabbitMQConstants.java`.
